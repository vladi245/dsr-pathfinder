Part 1 — From Undead Asylum to Blighttown
1. Northern Undead Asylum

1. Collect the key in your prison cell and escape.
2. Encounter the Asylum Demon. Escape through the side passage, or defeat it during this first encounter for the Demon's Great Hammer.
3. Retrieve your starting weapon and shield.
4. Avoid the boulder rolling down the staircase.
5. Speak with Oscar through the broken wall and accept the Estus Flask and East Key.
6. Unlock the shortcut to the courtyard bonfire.
7. Collect your starting spellcasting equipment if applicable.
8. Defeat the Asylum Demon, preferably opening with a plunging attack.
9. Collect the Big Pilgrim's Key and leave the asylum.
10. Collect the souls along the cliff path and travel to Firelink Shrine.

2. Firelink Shrine

1. Activate the bonfire and speak with the Crestfallen Warrior.
2. Speak with Petrus and obtain his Copper Coin.
3. Search Firelink for Humanity, souls, Firebombs, Homeward Bones, Morning Star, Talisman, Cracked Red Eye Orbs and Lloyd's Talismans.
4. Loot the graveyard for the Zweihander, Winged Spear, Caduceus Round Shield, Binoculars and souls.
5. Take the elevator to New Londo Ruins.
6. Meet Rickert and collect the Estoc, Transient Curses and nearby souls.
7. Retrieve the Fire Keeper Soul from New Londo.
8. Give it to Anastacia to upgrade your Estus Flask.
9. Visit the Valley of Drakes and retrieve Astora's Straight Sword and Dragon Crest Shield near the undead dragon.
10. Continue toward Darkroot Basin and activate its bonfire.
11. Defeat the Black Knight and collect the Grass Crest Shield.
12. Collect the Hunter Set, Longbow, arrows and Crystal Lizard materials.
13. Reach the Undead Parish bonfire above Andre and unlock the elevator connection to Firelink.
14. Retrieve the Undead Asylum West Key from Firelink's rooftop.
15. Return to the asylum using the giant crow and collect the Rusted Iron Ring.
16. Before entering Undead Burg, collect the Ring of Sacrifice and souls around the aqueduct.

3. Undead Burg

1. Enter the Burg and watch for archers and the Hellkite Drake.
2. Visit the Undead Merchant and buy useful supplies, especially the Residence Key, Repairbox and Bottomless Box.
3. Alternatively, kill the merchant for the Uchigatana and his drops, sacrificing his shop.
4. Collect Throwing Knives, Light Crossbow, bolts, Humanity, Rubbish and souls.
5. Retrieve the Wooden Shield from the room beneath the archer.
6. Activate the Undead Burg bonfire.
7. Find the Black Firebombs and Gold Pine Resin.
8. Defeat the Black Knight below the tower and collect the Blue Tearstone Ring.
9. Dodge the barrel trap on the tower staircase.
10. Break the barrels and kill the Crystal Lizard for upgrade materials.
11. Before the Taurus Demon, climb the tower and eliminate both archers.
12. Defeat the Taurus Demon using plunging attacks from the tower.
13. Collect the soul hidden behind crates.
14. Speak with Solaire and accept the White Sign Soapstone.
15. Reach the Hellkite bridge and descend the middle staircase.
16. Kick down the ladder to create a shortcut to the bonfire.
17. Shoot the Hellkite's tail from beneath the bridge to obtain the Drake Sword.
18. Collect the Claymore and souls from the bridge.
19. Reach the Sunlight Altar bonfire.
20. Join the Warrior of Sunlight covenant if eligible.
21. Open the route to Undead Parish.

4. Undead Parish

1. Defeat the Black Knight in the entrance tower.
2. Kill or evade the armored boar.
3. Collect the Mystery Key and nearby soul.
4. Defeat the armored knight inside the church and collect the Knight Shield.
5. Obtain the Basement Key and Halberd.
6. Open the main gate.
7. Activate the bonfire near Andre.
8. Speak with Andre, learn his gesture, buy upgrade supplies and reinforce your equipment.
9. Speak with Siegmeyer outside Sen's Fortress.
10. Defeat the Berenike Knight and collect the Fire Keeper Soul.
11. Upgrade your Estus Flask at Firelink if desired.
12. Defeat the Channeler and the hollows upstairs in the church.
13. Use the Mystery Key to free Lautrec.
14. Prepare for the Bell Gargoyles; Solaire and potentially Lautrec can assist.
15. Cut the first gargoyle's tail to obtain the Gargoyle Tail Axe.
16. Defeat both Bell Gargoyles.
17. Ring the first Bell of Awakening.
18. Meet Oswald, learn his gesture and buy useful items such as Purging Stones.
19. Return to the Hellkite bridge and unlock the entrance to Lower Undead Burg.

5. Lower Undead Burg

1. Unlock the shortcut connecting Lower Undead Burg to the upper Burg.
2. Use the Residence Key to rescue Griggs.
3. Break the barrels in his room to collect the Sorcerer Set and Sorcerer's Catalyst.
4. Explore the streets for Twin Humanities, Mail Breaker, Thief Set, Target Shield and souls.
5. Enter the Capra Demon arena and immediately head toward the stairs.
6. Eliminate the dogs before fighting the demon.
7. Defeat the Capra Demon and obtain the Key to Depths.
8. Visit the Female Undead Merchant.
9. Buy Purple Moss, Blooming Purple Moss, Transient Curses and Homeward Bones.
10. Open the shortcut to Firelink Shrine.
11. Speak with Griggs, the Crestfallen Warrior and Lautrec at Firelink.
12. Collect Lautrec's Sunlight Medal.
13. Decide whether to kill Lautrec early for the Ring of Favor and Protection or preserve his questline.
14. Return to the lower streets and enter the Depths.

6. Depths

1. Defeat the first Butcher and retrieve the Large Ember.
2. Free Laurentius by breaking the barrels around him.
3. Eliminate the second Butcher.
4. Watch for ceiling slimes while collecting nearby souls.
5. Activate the Depths bonfire.
6. Explore the Giant Rat area and collect the Spider Shield, Sewer Chamber Key and souls.
7. Defeat the Channeler before approaching the boss.
8. Collect the Greataxe and Large Titanite Shard.
9. Explore the lower sewers carefully to avoid Basilisk curse attacks.
10. Retrieve the Ring of the Evil Eye and Humanity.
11. Unlock the shortcut back to the bonfire.
12. Reverse hollowing and defeat Knight Kirk's invasion.
13. Collect the Heavy Crossbow and bolts near the boss route.
14. Summon Solaire or Lautrec if desired.
15. Cut off the Gaping Dragon's tail for the Dragon King Greataxe.
16. Defeat the Gaping Dragon and receive the Blighttown Key.
17. Collect the Warrior Set from the arena.
18. Meet Domhnall, learn his gesture and check his merchandise.
19. Ensure you have enough poison and toxin cures.
20. Unlock the entrance to Blighttown.

7. Blighttown and Quelaag's Domain

1. Carefully descend through Blighttown while eliminating Blowdart Snipers.
2. Jump across the wooden platforms to collect the Iaito.
3. Activate the upper Blighttown bonfire.
4. Collect Blooming Purple Moss and Humanity from nearby platforms.
5. Avoid the collapsing floor trap.
6. Retrieve the Shadow Set.
7. Eliminate the sniper overlooking the next ladder section.
8. Collect the Eagle Shield.
9. Defeat the Parasitic Wall Hugger from a distance.
10. Retrieve the Power Within pyromancy.
11. Collect the Whip while descending.
12. Continue toward the swamp, killing additional snipers.
13. Retrieve the Wanderer Set and Falchion.
14. Equip the Rusted Iron Ring before entering the swamp.
15. Activate the Blighttown Swamp bonfire.
16. Collect the Dragon Scale from the nearby chest.
17. Search the swamp for the Pyromancer Set, Poison Mist, Large Titanite Shards, Server and souls.
18. Explore the entrance to the Great Hollow for the Plank Shield and hidden Twin Humanities.
19. Defeat the boulder-throwing enemies to obtain the Great Club.
20. Optionally farm Giant Leeches for upgrade materials.
21. Climb the waterwheel and eliminate the snipers on the upper platforms.
22. Collect the Fire Keeper Soul near the dogs.
23. Return to the swamp bonfire and reverse hollowing.
24. Defeat invading Maneater Mildred for the Butcher Knife and Humanity.
25. Enter Quelaag's Domain and summon Mildred if desired.
26. Defeat Chaos Witch Quelaag.
27. Ring the second Bell of Awakening.
28. Reveal the hidden passage beneath the bell area.
29. Speak with Eingyi and gain access to the Daughter of Chaos.
30. Join the Chaos Servant covenant if desired.
31. Offer 30 Humanity to unlock the Lost Izalith shortcut later.
32. Upgrade your Estus Flask with the Daughter of Chaos if you have a Fire Keeper Soul.
33. Activate the Daughter of Chaos bonfire.
34. Find Quelana in Blighttown if she has appeared and purchase or upgrade pyromancies.
35. Return to the waterwheel and collect the Crimson Set, Tin Banishment Catalyst and Remedy.
36. Retrieve the Key to New Londo Ruins at the upper exit.
37. Enter the Valley of Drakes and optionally defeat the Undead Dragon.
38. Return to Firelink Shrine.
39. If Lautrec survived, collect the Black Eye Orb and Dingy Set from Anastacia's cell.
40. Speak with the Crestfallen Warrior, Griggs, Laurentius and Petrus.
41. Speak with Reah, Vince and Nico before their expedition.
42. Meet Kingseeker Frampt at Firelink.

Part 2 — Darkroot to Anor Londo

18. Give the Divine Ember to Andre.
19. Return to Firelink and check Petrus's dialogue regarding Reah.

21. If you joined the Forest Hunters, visit Shiva in Blighttown to purchase equipment and learn his gesture.

27. New Game Plus Preparation

1. Gather additional Humanity and kindle important bonfires.
2. Collect any remaining titanite and Crystal Lizard materials.
3. Purchase missing sorceries, miracles and pyromancies.
4. Upgrade weapons for the next playthrough.
5. Finish all remaining Snuggly trades.
6. Stock up on arrows, consumables and upgrade materials.
7. Purchase desired boss armor from Domhnall.
8. Optionally defeat remaining Fire Keepers for their souls.
9. Optionally collect blacksmith and merchant drops.
10. Return to Firelink and prepare for the final boss.

28. Kiln of the First Flame

1. Offer any remaining Lord Souls at Firelink Altar.
2. Enter the Kiln of the First Flame.
3. Farm Black Knights for their weapons and titanite.
4. Collect the Black Knight Set from the beam.
5. Prepare for the final battle.
6. Defeat Gwyn, Lord of Cinder.
7. Choose to link the fire by activating the bonfire, or leave the arena to become the Dark Lord.
8. Finish the playthrough and enter New Game Plus.

29. New Game Plus and Beyond

1. Offer Gwyn's soul at the Sunlight Altar while eligible for the Warrior of Sunlight covenant reward, Sunlight Spear.
2. Defeat Ornstein and Smough in the opposite order to obtain the other boss soul and associated rewards.
3. Collect additional boss souls to craft the remaining boss weapons.
4. Obtain a third Soul of Sif for all three Sif-related boss weapons and shields.
5. Retrieve any missed tail-cut weapons.
6. Farm Black Knights for their Sword, Greatsword, Greataxe, Halberd and Shield.
7. Farm Silver Knights for their Straight Sword, Spear and Shield.
8. Farm Stone Knights for the Stone Greatsword and Stone Greatshield.
9. Farm Sentinels for the Giant's Halberd.
10. Farm Channelers for the Channeler's Trident.
11. Complete any NPC quest branches, covenant rewards and achievements missed in the first playthrough.
